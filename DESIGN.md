# Design System: My Finance Register

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Aura com assinatura própria"**

My Finance Register should feel reliable and clear without imposing a decorative visual metaphor. Keep Vaadin Aura's familiar component language, light neutral surfaces, and readable hierarchy. Give the application a quiet signature through the teal and slate tones already present in its official logo.

**Key characteristics:**
- Aura's native component surfaces and interaction patterns remain the foundation.
- Teal is a restrained brand accent, not a large background fill.
- Financial values remain aligned and use semantic colors for positive and negative meaning.

## Colors

Use Aura's existing light background and surface palette. The logo's dominant teal `#087F83` is the primary accent; slate `#3D4C57` and navy `#2E3B5A` inform text and restrained structural details. Aura computes contrast, text, borders, and component states from its theme properties.

### Brand
- **Logo Teal (`#087F83`)**: Primary actions, focus, and subtle selection cues.
- **Logo Slate (`#3D4C57`)**: Brand reference for readable text and neutral hierarchy.
- **Logo Navy (`#2E3B5A`)**: A secondary reference only; avoid using it as a large navigation or page background.

### Semantic
- Keep Aura's green and red palette for positive and negative financial values and corresponding statuses.
- Mute Aura's default vivid blue to a slate-blue for any remaining informational use.
- Do not use gold, terracotta, or saturated blue as decorative brand colors.

## Typography

Use Aura's default system typography. Keep page titles clear and compact; use tabular numerals and consistent right alignment where financial amounts are compared.

## Layout

Keep the familiar application shell and Vaadin navigation. Place filters near the data they affect, maintain clear grouping and alignment in tables, and allow responsive layouts to wrap without changing the meaning or order of information.

## Elevation & Depth

Use Aura's native surface hierarchy and component elevation. Prefer spacing and subtle borders over decorative shadows or added texture.

## Shapes

Keep Aura's default component shapes. Avoid ornamental silhouettes, simulated paper, or custom shapes that compete with the data.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the official logo on a light Aura surface.
- **Do** reserve teal for actions and restrained emphasis.
- **Do** distinguish financial meaning with Aura's semantic colors and clear labels.
- **Do** retain Aura's native spacing, typography, and component behavior.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use saturated blue, gold, or terracotta as decoration.
- **Don't** create a dark navy shell or replace Aura's default light surfaces.
- **Don't** use color alone to communicate financial status.
